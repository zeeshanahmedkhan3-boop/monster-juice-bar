Monster Juice Bar: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Monster Juice Bar is a game for players of all ages, including children. It is built so that it does not need any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, age, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Internet access

The game works fully offline. It does not request the internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.

Game progress

Your progress (current day, coins and discovered recipes) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.

Ads, analytics and purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs, no in-app purchases and no links to social media.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play store page.
